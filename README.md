## 🎯 Clinical Problem & Business Objectives
Missed medical appointments lead to significant clinical backlogs, underutilized physician hours, and delayed treatments. This platform analyzes patient scheduling patterns to help hospital administrators:
* **Quantify Non-Attendance Drivers:** Identify demographic, geographic, and clinical factors correlating with missed visits.
* **Assess Communication Channels:** Evaluate the ROI and attendance lift generated by SMS appointment reminders.
* **Target High-Risk Cohorts:** Focus follow-up protocols on vulnerable age groups and specific medical neighborhoods.

---

## 💡 Key Analytical Features

* **Multi-Parameter Clinical Filters:** Dynamic filtering across Gender, Patient Age Ranges (0–95), Medical Neighbourhoods, and SMS Notification status.
* **Executive Performance Metrics (KPIs):** Instant visibility into **Total Appointments**, **Missed Appointments**, **No-Show Rate (%)**, and **Average Waiting Days**.
* **Interactive Visualizations (Plotly):**
  * **SMS Reminder Efficacy:** Segmented breakdown comparing attended vs. missed appointments across reminder recipients.
  * **Age Cohort Distribution:** Stacked age distribution highlighting non-attendance clusters across life stages.
  * **Chronic Conditions Impact:** Comparative analysis of patient attendance across Hypertension, Diabetes, and Alcoholism indicators.
  * **Neighbourhood Attendance Heatmap:** Horizontal risk ranking across regional medical districts.
* **Raw Records Inspector:** Interactive on-demand tabular preview of patient records.
